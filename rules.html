📜 Rules

How the draw works, how points are scored, and how ties are settled.

⚽ Entry & prizes

Entry is £10 per player. The current £200 pot is split:

🥇 1st: £110
🥈 2nd: £50
🥉 3rd: £25
🥄 Last: £15

🎲 The draw

  • Each player gets four teams.
  • Each player gets one team from each ranking pot.
  • Pots are based on team strength using FIFA rankings.
  • No player has two teams from the same World Cup group.
  • Every player’s four-team allocation is unique.

🏆 Scoring

EventPoints
Team wins a match+3
Team draws a match+1
Team keeps a clean sheet+2
Each goal scored+1
Each red card-1
Team wins its group+2
Team finishes bottom of its group-2

📊 Tiebreakers

If players are level on points, ties are settled in this order:

  1. Most wins
  2. Most goals scored
  3. Fewest goals conceded
  4. Fewest red cards

🥄 Wooden spoon

The player who finishes bottom of the final leaderboard wins the Wooden Spoon prize, and get £15 out of pity.
